PLATE 01

Colour — the four families

§02

Ivory · the surface scale · cool → warm

Perception test. The strip below is the same five values, adjacent and unlabelled. Maximum contrast ratio end-to-end is 1.04; adjacent steps are 1.011–1.026. No single step should be visible as a boundary. If you can see bands, the scale is wrong — that is the failure this test exists to catch.
Warmth (red minus blue) is monotonic: 2 → 6 → 11 → 19 → 22. The scale registers as temperature, not brightness — which is why it reads as passing time rather than as banded sections.

Ink · structure

Honey · the mark of resolution

Measured warning. honey-2 and honey-3 score 1.59 and 2.02 against ivory-3. They are fills, never inks, on any light ground. On ink-1 the same two score 11.35 and 8.94 — on dark they are unrestricted. This is the single most common way a gold brand goes wrong.

Slate · the unresolved

Quarantined to the noise register. Slate and honey are opposites in meaning, not merely in temperature: slate is the problem, honey is the resolution. The page loses its cold as it descends.

PLATE 10

Motion — curves, plotted and played

§07
--ease-settle is derived from the one piece of motion craft already on the site — the wordmark's alternating letter convergence with a long deceleration tail. That physics was right; it is now the law. Watch for the tail: mass coming to rest, never overshooting.

Duration scale — distance sets duration

Equal durations across unequal distances is why uniform fade-ups feel synthetic — the objects have no consistent mass. A 4px move takes 180ms; a 200px move takes 620ms.

The banned list

Four verbs exist: settle · shift · reveal · hold. Note what is absent — enter, appear, pop. Objects are not created by scrolling. The choreography budget shrinks down the page: 6 → 4 → 2 → 1 simultaneously moving elements. By the foot of the page, one thing moves, then nothing does.
PLATE 11

Interaction — the understructure & state contracts

§08

The understructure — move your pointer across the plate

the digital backbone your business grows on
Peak opacity 0.09, 260px radius, hex cell 84×150. It writes two CSS custom properties on pointer move and composites on its own layer — no layout, no reflow, no content repaint. Disabled below 1024px and on coarse pointers: there is no pointer to follow, and a decorative tap target teaches the user a lie about what is clickable. It is felt, not read — if you find yourself looking at the grid instead of the words, it is too strong. This is the only cursor-driven effect in the system.

State contracts — hover, press, and tab to these

Hover changes value, never position. The current hover:-translate-y-1.5 on cards violates Law 3 — surfaces are not magnetic and do not rise toward a pointer. Tab to the buttons above: the focus ring is honey-5, one of only five permitted honey marks, because focus genuinely is the most important thing on screen when it is present. Target floor is 44×44px, including inline links in dense text.

PLATE 12

Flat & opaque — two rules added by the coverage test

§02.13 · §06.5
Both rules exist because the Phase 1 coverage test found patterns in active use that no rule governed — gradients in 6 components, translucency in 5. That is what the coverage test is for.

Colour is flat

HIVECREST
✗ current — 5-stop gradient fill (PremiumHero.tsx:313)
HIVECREST
✓ flat --honey-3
A gradient is light that has no source. It belongs to the launch-day register — the reveal, the product shot, the hero glow — and it is the fastest way for a gold brand to read as ceremonial rather than engineered. Going flat also removes the per-letter background-clip:text workaround the current wordmark needs, because a gradient on a parent does not follow transformed children. Exceptions, both non-visible: mask-image and the understructure falloff — a gradient used as a tool, never as colour.

Surfaces are opaque

CONTENT SCROLLING BEHIND
✗ bg-ivory/85 + backdrop-blur-md — current nav
✓ opaque ground + 1px ink-6 hairline
Three reasons, in order of weight. ① A surface that shows what is behind it has no substance, and substance is the point. ② It cannot be contrast-audited — the measured ratio of text on a translucent ground depends on whatever happens to scroll behind it, which makes Rule 02.12 unenforceable. ③ backdrop-filter forces a full-screen composite every frame, and is one of the most reliable ways to drop frames on exactly the low-cost Android hardware this company's own copy promises to build for. A brand that says it builds for patchy connections and cheap Android phones cannot ship a frosted navigation bar. Sole exception: a full-screen modal scrim at --ink-1 / 55%, which carries no text and covers the viewport rather than compositing a strip.
